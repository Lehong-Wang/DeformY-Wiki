## Research areas

- Iterative Learning Control and locally-weighted learning for robot manipulation
- Dynamic manipulation of deformable objects on real hardware
- Sample-efficient learning with simplified dynamics models
- Long-running line on robot learning that bridges classical control theory and modern data-driven approaches

## Key papers

- [[learning-deformable-object-manipulation-using-task]] (2026, with Krishna Suresh) — senior author. Task-Level ILC with critical-point objectives for dynamic rope manipulation; 100% flying-knot success in ≤10 trials across 7 rope types.
