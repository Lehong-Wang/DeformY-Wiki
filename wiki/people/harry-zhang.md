## Research areas

Dynamic and self-supervised manipulation of deformable linear objects (cables, ropes, cords). Apex-point and low-dimensional action representations for high-speed arm motions. Real-robot self-supervised data collection on the UR5 platform.

## Key papers

- [[robots-lost-arc-self-supervised-learning]] — first author (ICRA 2022). Introduces INDy and the 3D-apex + min-jerk-QP parameterization for fixed-endpoint dynamic cable manipulation across vaulting, knocking, and weaving.

## My notes

Harry Zhang is a defining contributor to the AUTOLab dynamic-cable-manipulation line. The apex-point parameterization in INDy is his most cited contribution and remains a reference point for low-dimensional dynamic-manipulation action representations. The shared lineage with the same-year sister paper [[planar-robot-casting-real2sim2real-self-supervised]] from the AUTOLab is visible in the self-supervised data-collection pattern; the free-end successor [[self-supervised-learning-dynamic-planar-manipulation]] inherits the parameterization while relaxing the fixed-endpoint constraint.
