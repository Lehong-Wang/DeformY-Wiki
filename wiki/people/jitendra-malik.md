## Research areas

- [[sim-to-real-and-rapid-adaptation]]
Computer vision and, increasingly, embodied learning for robotics. Senior author on RMA, bringing the vision/representation-learning perspective (the adaptation module framed as learning the function from problem to solution, à la amortized inference) to legged-robot rapid adaptation.

## Recent work

- [[rma-rapid-motor-adaptation-legged-robots]] — senior author. RMA two-phase rapid motor adaptation for legged robots; first proprioception-only zero-shot deployment on the A1 across diverse terrains. RSS 2021.

## My notes

Malik's framing of the adaptation module as a learned surrogate for an optimization problem (system identification approximated by a neural map from interaction history to latent) is the conceptual seed of [[amortized-context-encoder-adaptation]] — the reusable idea this wiki extracts from RMA.
