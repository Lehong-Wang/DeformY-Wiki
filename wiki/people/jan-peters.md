## My notes

Senior author / advisor on [[ropedreamer-kinematic-recurrent-state-space-model]]. The paper sits at the intersection of TU Darmstadt's robot-learning lineage and Dreamer-family world models, with the novel contribution being the quaternionic kinematic-chain representation that enforces link-length constancy by construction. As the wiki ingests further DLO-manipulation papers from the IAS group, this page is the natural collation point.
