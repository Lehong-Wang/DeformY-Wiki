## My notes

Shuran Song's group is one of the leading academic labs on learning-based deformable-object manipulation. The IRP paper is one of several from this group that have shaped the modern dynamic-manipulation literature; her broader portfolio (FlingBot, Diffusion Policy, UMI) defines much of the state of the art. For DeformY, watching this group's follow-on work is a high-leverage signal for the field's direction.
