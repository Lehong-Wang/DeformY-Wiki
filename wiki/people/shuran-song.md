## Research areas

- Robot learning and manipulation
- Visual perception for robot action (3D scene understanding, descriptors, NeRF-based representations)
- Deformable-object manipulation (ropes, fabrics, garments)
- Dynamic manipulation (FlingBot, IRP)
- Sim-to-real transfer for visuomotor policies
- Generative robot policies (Diffusion Policy)

## Key papers

- [[iterative-residual-policy-goal-conditioned-dynamic]] (RSS 2022 Best Paper; IJRR 2024) — senior author. Introduces Iterative Residual Policy (IRP) for goal-conditioned dynamic manipulation of ropes and cloths.

## My notes

Shuran Song's group is one of the leading academic labs on learning-based deformable-object manipulation. The IRP paper is one of several from this group that have shaped the modern dynamic-manipulation literature; her broader portfolio (FlingBot, Diffusion Policy, UMI) defines much of the state of the art. For DeformY, watching this group's follow-on work is a high-leverage signal for the field's direction.
