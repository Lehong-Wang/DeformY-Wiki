## Research areas

- Dynamic manipulation of deformable linear objects (DLOs) such as ropes and cables.
- Soft-tool use: using ropes and cables as tools to manipulate rigid payloads.
- Heterogeneous soft-rigid system manipulation (rope + payload coupled systems).
- Implicit physics-aware policies and self-supervised sim-to-real learning for manipulation.

## Key papers

- [[implicit-physics-aware-policy-dynamic-manipulation]] — first author. ICRA 2025. Introduces the IPA policy and the heterogeneous-system formulation for one-shot dynamic transport via soft tools.

## Recent work

- IPA policy for heterogeneous DLO+rigid one-shot transport (ICRA 2025).
- Earlier related Purdue line on DeRi-Bot / DeRi-IGP (deformable-rigid bundling and grasp-pull moving primitives), referenced as background in IPA.
