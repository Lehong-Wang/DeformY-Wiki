# 2026-07-30

## What happened

No new research. A structural pass over knowledge produced 2026-07-24 → 07-29.

**Diagnosis first.** The wiki had 23 papers, 35 concepts, 6 topics, 5 methods and 43
foundations — and **zero ideas, zero experiments**. Every artifact of the project's own
research (base method, staged campaign, pre-registered task box, the two novelty deltas,
the retraction) lived in loose root markdown and `.agent/`. Nothing the repo's own tooling
operates on — `/ask`, `/exp-*`, `/novelty`, `neighbors`, `compile-context`, the Obsidian
graph — could see any of it.

**Entities created.** Idea `direction-conditioned-open-loop-rope-tip-targeting`
(status `in_progress`, priority 5, `origin_gaps` → 3 topics); methods
`per-timestep-hindsight-relabeling`, `smooth-basis-swing-parameterization`,
`conditional-flow-matching-motion-parameters`, `sim-verified-best-of-n-selection`,
`direction-reachability-atlas`; experiments `sim-stage-0-harness`,
`sim-stage-a-atlas-and-data-factory`, `sim-stage-b-amortization-shootout`,
`sim-stage-c-robustness-and-verifier-mismatch`, `sim-stage-d-gated-extensions`
(all `planned`). Stage-per-experiment granularity was chosen so each of the plan's five
exit criteria can gate and transition independently.

**Wiring.** Reverse links per `xref.yaml` (3 topics' `linked_ideas`, methods'
`## Evaluated by`, `ideas.linked_experiments`); 25 graph edges — 10 `derived_from` to
foundations, 12 `inspired_by` to papers, 3 `addresses_gap` to topics. `projected_edges.jsonl`
generated for the first time (213 rows from frontmatter links).

**Field knowledge distributed to where it will be found.** The 2026-07-28 scan's anchors and
gaps went into the three relevant topic pages rather than staying in `.agent/NOTES.md` only:
the direction-conditioning gap (re-confirmed), the *direction-coverage* gap (new, and the
harder half), the verifier-ranking-robustness gap, and the arm-side-fidelity gap.

**Root cleaned.** 8 `rope_swing_*.md` → `research/` + `README.md` status index. Intra-set
references are bare sibling filenames, so they survived the move untouched.

## Corrections made

- `.agent/ABOUT.md` still described the simulator as "~10K ropes @ 100 Hz". Corrected to
  the actual `DeformX/Cosserat-Rod-Sim-CUDA` facts (60 Hz control, 512–2048 envs). This was
  the last copy of the wrong number outside the ledger.
- `rope_swing_research_handover.md` — banner added: **superseded on method**. Its §3 commits
  to a meta-learned forward model + robust planner, which 2026-07-24/25 explicitly rejected.
  An agent reading it cold would have built the wrong thing.
- `rope_swing_field_report.md` — banner added: method framing stale, landscape facts valid.
- `Summary/dlo-dynamic-tip-targeting` Recommendations 1/4/5 contradicted current decisions —
  most sharply Rec 4, "build in online adaptation… 1–10 real refinement iterations", which
  the project's hard constraints forbid. Marked superseded in a table rather than rewritten,
  since the synthesis is a legitimate dated field record.
