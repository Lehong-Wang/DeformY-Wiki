# Field Report — Open-Loop, Zero-Shot Rope-Tip Targeting (Position + Direction)

**Audience:** PI. **Scope:** judge everything against OUR exact problem — a robot arm swings a flexible rope so the TIP reaches a 3D target position arriving along a specified unit DIRECTION, executed as ONE open-loop joint trajectory with NO feedback and NO per-target online adaptation; zero-shot after a one-time per-rope calibration; GPU sim + real arm + mocap; model-based (forward-model + meta-adaptation + robust planning + optional wind-up).

---

## (a) Executive Summary — where the field stands vs. OUR problem

**The blunt verdict: every individual block of our pipeline has a strong precedent, but NO published system instantiates our specific composition — and the precedents almost all live in a regime where the constraint we cannot relax (open-loop, no feedback) is the very thing they relax.** The literature's robust-planning, direction-constraint, and meta-adaptation results are overwhelmingly *closed-loop MPC* (table tennis, air hockey, dexterous hands), where continuous feedback silently corrects the model error that our open-loop throw must survive in one shot. This is not a minor caveat; it is the structural reason our problem is genuinely open.

What is **solved / directly borrowable**:
- **Compact action with hard endpoint position+velocity constraints**: BMP and CNP-B give us a spline/MP decoder that pins arrival direction *in closed form* — this is the single best-matched primitive in the entire set and should anchor the action representation.
- **Terminal velocity-DIRECTION as an explicit OCP constraint**: robot table tennis (Online Optimal Trajectory Generation, FHMPC, Spot table tennis) shows position + velocity-direction + surface-normal as independent terminal constraints. The *formulation* transfers cleanly even though the *closed-loop* execution does not.
- **RMA-for-a-forward-model meta-adaptation**: MAVEN (predictive context encoder), Dynamics-as-Prompts (gradient-free in-context SysID), IIDA, RoboMorph (one-shot whole-trajectory + in-context), TuneNet, and AdaptSim/ASID collectively prove the "sim prior → short real calibration → freeze" loop is feasible and gradient-free.
- **Anti-model-exploitation theory**: this is where the critic correction is load-bearing. The *theoretical backbone* the planning pillar was implicitly assuming — pessimism-from-uncertainty MDPs — is **MOReL / MOPO / COMBO / RAZER / DR-PETS**, and the planning pillar prose did NOT cite them. These are the literal foundation for "pessimism weight from calibration residual."
- **Open-loop verified reachability**: **the funnel / SOS-verification school (LQR-Trees, Invariant Funnels, Funnel Libraries, DIRTREL, Learning Provably Robust Motion Planners)** is the canonical, and previously-absent, answer to "guarantee an open-loop trajectory hits the target despite model uncertainty." A funnel library *IS* our feasibility map, and its lookup is zero-shot by construction. This was the single biggest discovery hole and is now filled.

What is **open / unsolved for us**:
1. **One-shot whole-trajectory forward model for high-speed, wave-dominated DLO dynamics.** No paper predicts an entire ballistic rope-tip path in one pass and then plans open-loop. The closest substrates are **Koopman/locally-linear DLO models** (Compositional Koopman, DLO Locally-Linear Latent Dynamics) and **operator learning (FNO/DeepONet — entirely absent and the most important missing method family)**.
2. **Arrival-DIRECTION through an UNDERACTUATED flexible tip under model uncertainty.** Everyone demonstrates direction control on a *rigid, directly-actuated* end effector at low/moderate speed. The terminal tip direction in our problem is a downstream consequence of the whole swing — nobody demonstrates this. Thinnest evidence base of all.
3. **Chaos / sensitivity of the open-loop throw.** Every pillar fears "small parameter error explodes by the tip" but no retrieved method *measures* it (Lyapunov/FTLE, reachability tubes). The funnel/reachability school is the right tooling but has never been applied to a chaotic whip.
4. **The integration itself** — co-training compact decoder + meta-context forward model + pessimistic offline planner as ONE open-loop system. Universally admitted as un-sourced; this is our novelty, but also our entire engineering risk.

**One-line takeaway:** the field hands us excellent isolated parts; our contribution is the open-loop, uncertainty-robust *composition* over a wave-dominated DLO, and the two methods most likely to de-risk it (operator-learning forward models + funnel/reachability verification of the feasibility map) were missing from the original survey and are the highest-value ingests.

## (b) The 7 Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Forward-model + offline planning (action → whole tip trajectory)
- **SOTA:** episodic-MP/spline decoders (BMP, Deep Black-Box RL w/ MP) + constraint-manifold decoders (CNP-B) + uncertainty-aware planners (RAZER, BaB-ND, DR-PETS, diffusion guidance). The *whole-trajectory, non-autoregressive* prediction is treated as near-novel.
- **Adopt directly:** BMP weight-space as the search variable; CNP-B manifold-decoding blueprint; BaB-ND as the GPU-parallel global-bounding search engine; RAZER's epistemic/aleatoric split as the cost.
- **Missing:** the actual forward-model substrate for a wave-dominated rope. The pillar prose outruns its evidence — none of the cited titles predict a high-speed DLO tip path in one shot. **Operator learning (FNO/DeepONet)** is the natural fit (the tip path is a *functional* of the actuation signal) and is entirely absent; **Koopman DLO models** give a globally/locally-linear forward model amenable to QP planning and were also missing.
- **Most actionable recommendation:** Prototype TWO forward-model heads on identical sim data and pick by *direction-error calibration*, not MSE: (i) a **Koopman/locally-linear latent head** (Compositional Koopman / DLO Locally-Linear Latent Dynamics) for a provably-robust QP planner, and (ii) an **operator-learning or seq2seq one-shot head** (FNO/DeepONet, or a RoboMorph-style transformer) for expressivity. Plan over whichever is better-calibrated with a RAZER/MOPO uncertainty penalty in BMP weight space.

### Pillar 2 — Meta-learned forward model + rapid per-rope adaptation
- **SOTA:** RMA/MAVEN predictive context encoders; in-context SysID transformers (Dynamics-as-Prompts); Bayesian real-to-sim; Fisher-information calibration design (ASID); task-driven fitting (AdaptSim). RoboMorph adds the missing piece: in-context meta-learning that emits a *whole* trajectory in one pass.
- **Adopt directly:** MAVEN explicit-prediction encoder loss (we have dense mocap GT); Dynamics-as-Prompts gradient-free calibration; ASID Fisher-information criterion to *design* the calibration swings; AdaptSim task-driven objective (fit for tip pos+dir, not whole-trajectory MSE); RoboMorph context-fraction design; TuneNet one-shot residual tuning; **MetaKoopman** closed-form MNIW Bayesian update (no gradients → maps perfectly to "frozen after calibration").
- **Missing:** all DLO real-to-sim is quasi-static; **fast/inertial deformable sysID (aerodynamic drag, damping, modal/frequency-domain identification of flexible links)** is absent — yet a "wiggle probe" IS a frequency sweep, and modal-ID controls theory is exactly how to reason about which modes it excites. **Vid2Sid** (Cosserat-rod, video-driven, handles the high-speed-oscillation regime) is the closest real precedent and should be ingested.
- **Most actionable recommendation:** Build an RMA-for-forward-model encoder supervised with MAVEN-style explicit next-state loss, with calibration swings DESIGNED by ASID Fisher-information, and a **modal/frequency-domain identification** layer to extract damping/drag at speed (the inertial-regime prior the DLO literature lacks). Cross-check parameter identifiability with the Bayesian-real-to-sim multi-modal posterior to flag direction-ambiguous ropes BEFORE deployment.

### Pillar 3 — Compact smooth action representation (min-jerk via-point spline)
- **SOTA:** BMP (closed-form endpoint position+velocity pinning), CNP-B (B-spline on a constraint manifold), Bezier/ProDMP (Spot table tennis), 5D task-space decomposition (RSS table tennis case study), shooting-angle 1D reduction (distilled air hockey), VAE skill-latents (D-Cubed).
- **Adopt directly:** BMP as the decoder — it is the *only* representation that pins BOTH endpoint position and endpoint velocity in closed form, encoding arrival direction structurally rather than as a soft penalty. The min-jerk two-phase (backward-then-forward) layout is empirically grounded by the BioRob whip papers.
- **Missing:** nothing critical — this is the best-served pillar. The only gap is that no decoder couples its weight prior to an uncertainty-aware planner over a *meta-conditioned* model (our burden).
- **Most actionable recommendation:** Lock BMP as the action; make its low-dimensional Gaussian weight vector the planner's search space. This single choice converts the direction constraint from a fragile penalty into a hard structural guarantee and gives a well-conditioned manifold for pessimistic search.

### Pillar 4 — Robust / anti-exploitation cost-guided planning (THE dominant risk)
- **SOTA:** the prose cited JRD-PETS, BaB-ND, diffusion guidance — but the critic correctly flags that the *theoretical foundation* was un-sourced. The real SOTA stack is **MOReL/MOPO** (uncertainty-penalized P-MDP, lower-bounds true return), **COMBO** (conservatism in the value function), **RAZER** (epistemic/aleatoric split inside CEM — the closest algorithmic match to our design), **DR-PETS** (Wasserstein-ambiguity distributional robustness), plus **CVaR/axiomatic risk** (Majumdar) and **conformal-prediction tubes** (WCP/L4DC) for finite-sample real-rope guarantees.
- **Adopt directly:** RAZER as the planner skeleton; MOPO's scalar `r − λ·u(s,a)` as the concrete penalty; MOReL's known/unknown partition as the trust-region definition; CVaR over the ensemble (not mean, not worst-case) as the principled risk objective; conformal tubes to calibrate λ from the *actual* calibration residual; Tube-Acceleration's success-tube for wind-up timing jitter.
- **Missing:** every cited robust-planning result is closed-loop and validated on quasi-static/rigid contact — UNPROVEN whether ensemble pessimism is calibrated enough on chaotic whip dynamics. No chaos/sensitivity analysis at all.
- **Most actionable recommendation:** Make anti-exploitation first-class: score each BMP candidate as (tip-position error + arrival-direction angle error + epistemic penalty), REJECT any candidate whose **CVaR-over-ensemble** worst case breaches a trust region, and **set the pessimism weight λ from the conformal-prediction coverage of the calibration residual** (this is the MOPO/MOReL principle the original prose was missing). Use BaB-ND or batch-parallel argmin (KCGG/Diffusion-ES) as the GPU search engine.

### Pillar 5 — Optional wind-up into a stable limit cycle (Hopf)
- **SOTA:** OSMPs (learned Hopf-bifurcation limit cycle with formal orbital stability + task conditioning); the BioRob "Preparatory Movements" paper proves empirically that a backward counter-movement *substantially expands the reachable workspace* by energy storage/release.
- **Adopt directly:** OSMP's supercritical Hopf radial dynamics as a *provably repeatable* open-loop initial condition; OSMP's scalar task-conditioning `z` for spanning the feasibility map without per-target retraining; the BioRob min-jerk two-phase prior for via-point layout.
- **Missing:** OSMP is demonstrated on quasi-static periodic tasks (UR5, soft helix, turtle swimmer), not on ballistic energy-storage whip swings; no work composes a wind-up limit cycle into a one-shot throw while *preserving a robustness certificate* — except, crucially, the **funnel sequential-composition theory** (Funnel Libraries), which tells us exactly how to chain a limit cycle into a swing primitive while keeping the certificate valid.
- **Most actionable recommendation:** Treat wind-up as optional-but-instrumented: implement OSMP for the limit cycle, but compose it into the swing using funnel sequential-composition so the wind-up's timing jitter is absorbed inside a verified funnel rather than degrading an unverified open-loop release.

### Pillar 6 — Feasibility map = reachable (position × direction) set
- **SOTA (after correction):** this pillar's evidence was the thinnest until the funnel/reachability school surfaced. The real SOTA is **Funnel Libraries** (a pre-computed funnel library literally IS a feasibility map with robustness certificates and zero-shot lookup), **DeepReach** (scalable HJ-reachability / backward reachable tubes for high-D nonlinear systems), **REFINE** (control-parameterized zonotope FRS, offline-compute/online-lookup), the **velocity-hedgehog + BRT** throwing precedent (precomputes max speed per direction → directly analogous to our position×direction set), and **LQR-Trees / Learning Provably Robust Motion Planners** (PAC-Bayes certificates that transfer to new settings).
- **Adopt directly:** REFINE's offline-FRS / online-parameter-lookup template (swap vehicle dynamics → arm+rope, collision sets → tip-direction cones); DeepReach to compute the disturbance-aware reachable set; the velocity-hedgehog data structure for the direction axis; PAC-Bayes funnel learning to certify the post-calibration plan.
- **Missing:** none of these has been applied to a deformable chain whose direction is a downstream tip consequence; the parameter-sensitivity Jacobian is high-dimensional and the informative/large motions may be unsafe.
- **Most actionable recommendation:** Build the feasibility map as a **funnel library / control-parameterized reachable set** over BMP via-point parameters, computed offline in sim and certified post-calibration via PAC-Bayes. This makes the deliverable a verified lookup table, satisfies zero-shot-at-runtime by construction, and is the cleanest answer to open-loop fragility.

### Pillar 7 — Sim-to-real for high-speed DLO (incl. wind, drag, energy dissipation)
- **SOTA:** Vid2Sid (Cosserat-rod, video-driven SysID, sub-13% param error, explicitly handles fast oscillation); RSS table-tennis latency modeling (per-modality Gaussian latency — critical and directly applicable to our mocap pipeline); domain-randomization-optimization (DROPO/DiffCloud) for param fitting; FLASH (GPU-native deformable solver).
- **Adopt directly:** Vid2Sid calibration protocol and parameter exposure list (damping/stiffness/material — the unknowns that dominate a swung rope); RSS per-modality latency interpolation for camera/encoder frequency mismatch; FLASH solver-architecture ideas for our parallel sim.
- **Missing:** the entire **inertial-regime deformable sysID literature** — aerodynamic drag/casting models (fishing-rod casting dynamics), soft-robot inertial sysID, and frequency-domain/modal identification of flexible links. All retrieved DLO real-to-sim is quasi-static; "what does a few-minute calibration actually identify at whip speed" is genuinely under-evidenced.
- **Most actionable recommendation:** Add an explicit **drag + damping identification stage** using frequency-domain/modal analysis of a calibration wiggle-sweep (treat the wiggle probe as a frequency sweep), and adopt Vid2Sid's Cosserat-rod parameter set as the calibration target. This is the inertial-regime prior the field has not packaged for us.

## (c) Newest & most important finds (beyond the known survey set)

The standouts are the ones the original discovery MISSED — they extend, not confirm, the frontier:

1. **Funnel / SOS-verification school (LQR-Trees 2010, Invariant Funnels 2011, Funnel Libraries 2017, DIRTREL 2017, Learning Provably Robust Motion Planners 2021).** Why it matters: this is the canonical answer to "verified open-loop execution under model uncertainty," and a funnel library *is* our feasibility map with zero-shot lookup and robustness certificates. For an open-loop throw where one exploited prediction is unrecoverable, this is arguably the single most important missing methodology.
2. **Offline-MBRL pessimism theory (MOReL, MOPO, COMBO, RAZER, DR-PETS).** Why it matters: these are the literal theoretical foundation for "pessimism weight from calibration residual" — the planning pillar's central claim was previously un-cited. MOPO gives the concrete penalty formula; RAZER is the closest algorithmic match; DR-PETS gives distributional-robustness certificates against the *structured* sim-to-real perturbation that is exactly our gap.
3. **Koopman DLO forward models (Compositional Koopman 2020, DLO Locally-Linear Latent Dynamics 2022, DeSKO 2022, MetaKoopman 2025).** Why it matters: a globally/locally-linear rope forward model enables open-loop planning via a single QP and provable robust planning; DLO Locally-Linear is *rope-specific with an open-loop tip-targeting sampler*; MetaKoopman fuses meta-learning + Koopman + closed-form Bayesian calibration into one frame matching three of our pillars.
4. **Reachability tooling (DeepReach 2021, REFINE 2022, velocity-hedgehog/BRT throwing 2022, conformal-prediction tubes/WCP 2024, CC-VPSTO 2024).** Why it matters: builds the feasibility map AND bounds worst-case tip trajectories; CC-VPSTO is *via-point chance-constrained optimization* — almost exactly our action representation under uncertainty.
5. **Direction-constraint OCP precedent (Online Optimal Trajectory Generation for Table Tennis 2018).** Why it matters: canonical hard terminal velocity-direction equality constraint `J(q(T))q̇(T)=v_des(T)` and contact-inversion (desired post-impact direction → required pre-impact arm state) — the cleanest template for our direction requirement.
6. **RoboMorph (2024) + Vid2Sid (2026) + MAVEN (2026).** Why they matter: RoboMorph is the rare *one-shot whole-trajectory + in-context meta-learning* on a real arm; Vid2Sid is Cosserat-rod-specific fast-oscillation calibration; MAVEN is the cleanest predictive-context-encoder pattern.

## (e) Concrete recommendations mapped to our pipeline + prioritized ingest list

**Pipeline mapping (the recommended stack):**
- **Action:** BMP spline decoder (closed-form endpoint position+velocity → direction as a hard constraint).
- **Forward model:** RMA-for-forward-model context encoder (MAVEN-style explicit loss) feeding a one-shot whole-trajectory head; prototype BOTH a Koopman/locally-linear head AND an operator-learning/seq2seq head; select by direction-error calibration.
- **Calibration:** ASID Fisher-information-designed swings + modal/frequency-domain drag/damping ID (Vid2Sid Cosserat params); gradient-free (Dynamics-as-Prompts / MetaKoopman closed-form Bayesian update); frozen after.
- **Planner:** CVaR/epistemic-penalized search in BMP weight space (RAZER + MOPO penalty, λ set from conformal-prediction calibration residual), BaB-ND/batch-argmin as the GPU engine; reject candidates breaching a trust-region funnel.
- **Wind-up:** OSMP Hopf limit cycle composed via funnel sequential-composition.
- **Feasibility map:** funnel library / REFINE-style control-parameterized reachable set, certified post-calibration via PAC-Bayes; velocity-hedgehog structure for the direction axis.

**Prioritized NEW papers to INGEST next (highest → lowest leverage):**
1. **Funnel Libraries for Real-Time Robust Feedback Motion Planning (2017)** — feasibility map = funnel library; verified open-loop; the #1 missing methodology.
2. **MOPO (2020)** + **MOReL (2020)** — the un-cited theoretical foundation for pessimism-from-calibration-residual; concrete penalty formula.
3. **RAZER: Risk-Averse Zero-Order Trajectory Optimization (2021)** — closest algorithmic match to our planner; epistemic/aleatoric split.
4. **DLO Prediction Using Locally Linear Latent Dynamics (2022)** — rope-specific one-shot tip prediction + open-loop sampler; our forward-model substrate.
5. **BMP (2024)** — the action decoder (if not already ingested as a known item, ingest now for the closed-form direction pinning).
6. **Online Optimal Trajectory Generation for Table Tennis (2018)** — canonical hard terminal velocity-direction constraint + contact inversion.

9. **MetaKoopman (2025)** — meta + Koopman + closed-form Bayesian calibration in one frame.

## (f) Open gaps & risks vs. how the literature does/doesn't address them

- **Model exploitation (DOMINANT risk).** *Addressed in theory, untested in our regime.* MOReL/MOPO/COMBO/RAZER/DR-PETS give pessimism-from-uncertainty MDPs and CVaR/Wasserstein objectives; conformal tubes calibrate the penalty from real residuals. BUT every validation is quasi-static/rigid and closed-loop. UNPROVEN that ensemble pessimism is calibrated on chaotic whip dynamics where parameter error explodes by the tip. *Mitigation:* set λ from conformal coverage of the calibration residual; verify with funnel/reachability rather than trusting the ensemble alone.
- **Direction-conditioning (our differentiator).** *Weakest evidence base.* Direction-as-OCP-constraint exists only on rigid, directly-actuated, low-speed effectors (table tennis, air hockey). No one hits a velocity-direction target with an underactuated flexible tip whose terminal direction is a downstream swing consequence. *Mitigation:* BMP closed-form velocity pinning + contact-inversion (2018 table-tennis) + a direction-error-calibrated forward-model selection; flag direction-ambiguous ropes via the Bayesian multi-modal posterior.
- **High-speed sim2real.** *Partially addressed.* Vid2Sid (Cosserat, fast oscillation) and latency modeling help; but inertial-regime drag/damping/modal ID is missing from the candidate set. *Mitigation:* add a modal/frequency-domain ID stage; ingest the controls flexible-link-vibration literature.
- **Open-loop fragility.** *Best-addressed by the previously-missing funnel/reachability school.* Funnels/BRT/zonotope-FRS certify which targets are reachable and how much perturbation still lands the tip in the target cone — without any online adaptation, matching our hard constraint exactly. *Mitigation:* make the feasibility map a verified funnel library; chain wind-up via sequential composition.
- **Chaos / sensitivity.** *Unaddressed methodologically.* No retrieved work measures finite-time sensitivity for the failure mode every pillar fears. *Mitigation:* add FTLE/Lyapunov-exponent sensitivity analysis to the calibration stage; treat high-sensitivity (position×direction) cells as infeasible in the map.
- **The integration gap.** *Universally un-sourced (good for novelty, bad for risk).* No paper co-trains decoder + meta-context forward model + pessimistic planner as one open-loop system. The closest end-to-end analogs (offline-MBRL joint dynamics+planning; differentiable-MPC/Universal Planning Networks) were absent and should be pulled as design references for the joint-training objective.

## Appendix — Per-Pillar Syntheses (raw)

### Forward-model + offline planning (action -> whole tip trajectory; avoid mode-averaging; plan per target)

**State of the art.** The strongest current recipe for our exact setting is a learned forward model planned over offline, with three converging design families. (1) Episodic/whole-trajectory forward models: instead of autoregressive step-by-step prediction (which compounds error and invites model exploitation), the trajectory is parameterized as a compact movement primitive (DMP/ProMP/ProDMP, B-spline) and a single forward pass maps that parameter vector to the entire outcome — Deep Black-Box RL with Movement Primitives and BMP show this reshapes sparse, non-Markovian terminal rewards (hit a 3D point along a direction) into well-conditioned objectives, exactly our regime. (2) Constraint-manifold decoders: CNP-B (T-RO 2024) bakes the reachable (position x velocity-direction) set directly into a B-spline-decoding network so one inference yields a feasible, constraint-satisfying joint trajectory — a near-isomorphic precedent for our feasibility map plus decoder. (3) Robust/anti-exploitation planners over the frozen model: PETS-style probabilistic ensembles with explicit pessimism remain the canonical baseline (GC-PMPC goal-conditions it; JRD-ensemble RSS 2023 replaces naive variance with a principled Jensen-Renyi epistemic penalty), while diffusion planners (Diffuser, MetaDiffuser, D-Cubed, Diffusion-ES, KCGG) add a learned trajectory prior that keeps cost-guided search on-manifold, and BaB-ND (ICLR 2025) brings branch-and-bound global bounding that provably resists the spurious optima CEM/MPPI chase. Across all three, the dominant theme is decouple a fast, compact, one-shot trajectory generator from a cost-guided search that is explicitly uncertainty-aware, evaluated offline, and executed open-loop.

**Directly usable:**
- BMP (Bridging B-Spline and Movement Primitives, 2024): spline decoder that pins endpoint position AND velocity in closed form — encodes arrival-direction directly into the action space; search its low-dim Gaussian weight space.
- Deep Black-Box RL with Movement Primitives (CoRL 2022): episodic-MP parameterization + differentiable trust-region layer — justifies optimizing one trajectory-param vector against a terminal success cost and gives a drop-in trust-region step.
- CNP-B / Fast Kinodynamic Planning on the Constraint Manifold (T-RO 2024): blueprint for a network that maps (target position, arrival direction) to a B-spline in one pass while baking in the feasibility manifold.
- PETS-style probabilistic ensemble with JRD epistemic penalty (Bridging Active Exploration..., RSS 2023): drop-in pessimism signal (Jensen-Renyi divergence) for the planning cost — better than naive ensemble variance.
- GC-PMPC (Multi-Goal Dexterous, 2025): goal-conditioned ensemble + pessimistic planning template; precedent for ~80-min real specialization budget.
- BaB-ND (ICLR 2025): branch-and-bound global objective bounding over a frozen neural dynamics model — GPU-parallel, resists CEM/MPPI spurious optima; validated on rope routing.
- Tube Acceleration (T-RO 2024): success-tube formulation — optimize a trajectory family robust across timing/release jitter; directly maps to open-loop wind-up timing robustness.
- MetaDiffuser (ICML 2023) dual-guided sampling: dynamics-consistency classifier + return guidance — the anti-exploitation mechanism for a diffusion variant of the planner.
- D-Cubed (2024) CEM-inside-reverse-diffusion: simulator-cost-guided denoising without gradients; validated on deformable (rope) tasks.
- KCGG / Diffusion-ES (2025/2024): whole-trajectory gradient guidance and manifold-preserving evolutionary mutation — gradient-free, batch-parallel argmin selection as an exploitation-resistant CEM replacement.
- TD-MPC2 (ICLR 2024): decoder-free latent world model reduces compounding error — architectural reference for predicting the whole tip trajectory in latent space.

**Key gaps.** No candidate demonstrates a forward model that predicts a WHOLE high-speed tip trajectory in one shot for a ballistic/wave-dominated DLO and then plans open-loop zero-shot — every robust-planning result here is closed-loop MPC (table tennis, air hockey, dexterous hands, MPPI-in-sim) where feedback continuously corrects model error, which is exactly the crutch our open-loop constraint removes. The anti-model-exploitation methods (JRD-PETS, BaB-ND, diffusion guidance) are validated on quasi-static or rigid contact, not on the chaotic-sensitive regime of whip dynamics where small parameter error explodes by the tip — so it is UNPROVEN whether ensemble pessimism is calibrated enough to prevent a confident-but-wrong open-loop throw. The arrival-DIRECTION constraint is handled by everyone only at low tip speed (CNP-B air-hockey, table-tennis paddle normal); none demonstrate hitting a velocity-direction target with an underactuated flexible tip whose terminal direction is a downstream consequence of the whole swing, not a directly actuated quantity. Finally, no method co-trains the compact-action decoder, the meta-learned (RMA-context) forward model, and the pessimistic offline planner as one system — the literature gives us each block in isolation, and the integration (especially making BMP's Gaussian weight prior the search space for an uncertainty-aware planner over a meta-conditioned model) is our novel burden.

**How it helps us.** Adopt a two-block, anti-exploitation-first architecture: (1) a BMP / ProDMP spline as the compact action because it is the only representation in this set that pins BOTH endpoint position and endpoint velocity in closed form, directly encoding the arrival-direction constraint into the search space instead of as a soft penalty; (2) plan over a FROZEN probabilistic ensemble forward model (action-params -> whole tip trajectory) with cost-guided gradient-free search and a hard pessimism term — never a single nominal model. The single most actionable move: treat anti-model-exploitation as a first-class objective by scoring each BMP-weight candidate as (tip-position error + arrival-direction angle error + JRD epistemic penalty) and rejecting any candidate whose WORST-CASE-over-ensemble cost exceeds a trust-region threshold, borrowing Tube-Acceleration's success-tube idea so we optimize for a trajectory robust across both ensemble disagreement and wind-up timing jitter. Use BaB-ND-style global bounding or batch-parallel argmin (KCGG/Diffusion-ES) as the GPU-parallel search engine, since open-loop execution makes a single optimistic exploit unrecoverable at runtime. This keeps the entire pipeline offline, zero-shot per target, and aligned with our massively parallel simulator while squarely attacking the dominant risk.

### Meta-learned forward model and rapid per-rope adaptation (sim prior + few-min real; context-encoder / RMA-for-a-forward-model; frozen)

**State of the art.** The strongest current recipe is a sim-trained, context-conditioned dynamics model specialized to the real system from a short interaction log and then frozen, exactly the RMA template (Kumar et al.) generalized from a control policy to a forward model. Three encoder families exist: (1) RMA/Nagabandi-style latent context encoders regressing an environment embedding from a sliding window of recent transitions (MAVEN sharpens this with explicit one-step dynamics+reward prediction losses and a permutation-invariant MLP over a transition buffer, giving zero-shot frozen transfer to large unseen dynamics shifts); (2) in-context SysID with a causal transformer that reads a real trajectory window and emits corrected simulator parameters with no gradient updates (Dynamics-as-Prompts), the cleanest fit to a one-time calibration budget; and (3) principled real-to-sim parameter inference for deformables, either Bayesian/likelihood-free (A Bayesian Treatment of Real-to-Sim, multi-modal posteriors over elasticity/friction from short real sequences) or differentiable (DiffCloud). Orthogonally, the field makes calibration data-efficient and transfer-aligned: ASID uses Fisher-information-maximizing exploration policies trained in sim so one informative real episode suffices for parameter recovery, while AdaptSim and Incremental Few-Shot Adaptation optimize simulator parameters for downstream task performance rather than raw trajectory-matching. MetaDiffuser shows the context embedding can directly condition a generative planner, unifying this pillar with cost-guided planning. Net SOTA: explicit-prediction context encoder (RMA/MAVEN) or in-context SysID transformer as the adaptation mechanism, fed by Fisher-information-designed calibration motions, with task-driven parameter fitting, all frozen before deployment.

**Directly usable:**
- RMA (Kumar et al.) base recipe: sim-trained context encoder + frozen-after-calibration deployment, generalized from policy to a one-shot forward model action->tip-trajectory
- MAVEN predictive context encoder: permutation-invariant MLP over a transition buffer with explicit dynamics+reward prediction losses, zero-shot frozen transfer to large dynamics shifts
- Dynamics-as-Prompts: causal SysID transformer reads a real trajectory window and outputs corrected simulator rope params with NO gradient updates at calibration time
- ASID: Fisher-information-maximizing sim-trained exploration policy to design maximally-informative calibration swings; one short real episode suffices for parameter recovery
- A Bayesian Treatment of Real-to-Sim: likelihood-free Bayesian inference of rope elasticity/friction from short real sequences, yielding multi-modal posteriors (flags physical ambiguity)
- AdaptSim / Incremental Few-Shot Adaptation: tune simulator parameters for task-performance (tip pos+dir accuracy) not raw trajectory-matching; converges in <10 real interactions
- Nagabandi Learning-to-Adapt: meta-learned dynamics model as the forward-model prior, online context inference (we use it once at calibration then freeze)
- MetaDiffuser: inject the calibration context embedding into a cost-guided diffusion planner to unify this pillar with robust planning
- DiffCloud / DROPO: differentiable-sim and offline domain-randomization-optimization alternatives for gradient-based rope param fitting from mocap

**Key gaps.** None of these target high-speed BALLISTIC rope/whip dynamics: all deformable real-to-sim work (Bayesian Real-to-Sim, DiffCloud, Incremental Few-Shot) is quasi-static cloth/pushing, where stiffness/damping identifiability is very different from the wave-propagation, energy-storage regime of a swung rope; whether a few-minute calibration can pin down the parameters that govern tip arrival is unproven. No paper validates a context encoder feeding a ONE-SHOT whole-trajectory forward model (action-spline -> entire tip path) under meta-adaptation; existing context encoders condition autoregressive/step-based models or policies, so compounding-error behavior of our one-shot map under a frozen z is untested. The multi-modal posteriors in the Bayesian work warn that rope parameters may be only partially identifiable from short data, which directly threatens zero-shot accuracy on the DIRECTION constraint (arrival direction is far more sensitive to dynamics error than position). ASID's Fisher-information design is shown for rigid sphere/rod tasks, not for a deformable chain where the parameter-sensitivity Jacobian is high-dimensional and the informative motions may be unsafe/large. Finally, no work couples a frozen meta-learned forward model with anti-exploitation planning, leaving open whether residual sim-to-real error in f gets amplified by the planner optimizing tip position+direction.

**How it helps us.** Adopt an RMA-for-a-forward-model architecture for our exact constraint: train, in the GPU sim across randomized rope physics (linear density, bending/twisting stiffness, damping, length), a context encoder phi mapping a short window of real mocap tip/segment trajectories to a latent z, and a one-shot forward model f(action-spline-params, z) -> whole tip trajectory; the few-minute per-rope calibration runs phi on real swings, then z is frozen and never updated per target, exactly satisfying open-loop zero-shot. Supervise phi with MAVEN-style explicit next-state prediction loss on real tip trajectories (not an implicit/critic signal), since our calibration has dense mocap ground truth and direct prediction is far more sample-efficient and identifiable. Use ASID's Fisher-information criterion to DESIGN the calibration swings: plan sim swings whose tip trajectories are maximally sensitive to the unknown rope parameters, so a handful of real swings saturates the budget with maximal information. Make the fitting objective task-driven (AdaptSim): condition for tip position+direction prediction accuracy at the swing endpoint, not whole-trajectory MSE, since only terminal tip state and arrival direction define success. For a gradient-free path, the Dynamics-as-Prompts SysID transformer is a strong drop-in reading the real window and emitting corrected sim parameters with zero gradient steps.

### Compact smooth action parameterization (min-jerk via-point splines, DMP/ProMP/MMP, learned low-dim trajectory decoders)

**State of the art.** The strongest current pattern is episodic / one-shot trajectory parameterization: instead of per-step control, the entire motion is emitted as a low-dimensional parameter vector (spline control points or movement-primitive weights) decoded into a smooth joint trajectory, with terminal position AND velocity-direction enforced as hard boundary conditions. B-spline decoders that map a (target-position, arrival-direction) tuple to control points in a single network forward pass are now proven on real high-speed hardware (CNP-B air hockey, Bezier/SQP quadrupedal table tennis, FHMPC table-tennis swinging), all of which independently specify terminal position, velocity-direction angle, and surface/normal orientation as separate constraints — structurally identical to our tip position+direction goal. BMP (2024) unifies B-splines with movement primitives, giving a probabilistic weight distribution over smooth curves while pinning both endpoint position and endpoint velocity in closed form (no penalty terms), which is the cleanest known decoder for direction-constrained throwing. On the learning side, Deep Black-Box RL with Movement Primitives (CoRL 2022) shows that sparse, non-Markovian, terminal-only rewards — exactly our tip success metric — become well-shaped under episodic-MP parameterization and break step-based RL, and it pairs this with a differentiable trust-region layer for stable optimization in weight space. Latent decoders (D-Cubed VAE skill latents, TD-MPC2 implicit latent) extend the idea to learned low-dim composition of primitives. Biomechanics work (whip min-jerk two-phase optimization, center-of-mass surrogate, OSMP Hopf primitives) confirms that high-DOF underactuated whip/rope targeting is well-captured by a compact min-jerk via-point representation with an optional preparatory (wind-up) phase.

**Directly usable:**
- BMP (B-spline Movement Primitives, 2024): drop-in spline decoder that closed-form pins endpoint position AND velocity — use for arm joint trajectory so tip arrival-direction is structurally enforceable.
- Deep Black-Box RL with Movement Primitives (CoRL 2022): episodic MP parameterization makes our sparse terminal tip-success reward well-shaped; reuse its differentiable trust-region layer as the planner stabilizer + anti-exploitation guard.
- CNP-B / Fast Kinodynamic Planning on the Constraint Manifold (T-RO 2024): blueprint for a single-forward-pass network mapping (position, velocity-direction) -> B-spline control points; also validates our (position x direction) feasibility-map as a learned constraint manifold.
- FHMPC table-tennis swinging (2025): terminal-constraint OCP that separately specifies arrival position + velocity-direction angle + orientation; its sliding timing-index trick can absorb wind-up timing jitter in open-loop execution without re-solving.
- Whole-Body Spin-Aware Quadrupedal Table Tennis (ICRA 2026): concrete Bezier (8 control-points/joint) + SQP instantiation showing fast strike-point-constrained spline optimization.
- Human-Inspired Whip Control (BioRob 2024): min-jerk two-phase (backward windup -> forward strike) via-point layout as a human-grounded prior for our spline knot placement.
- D-Cubed (2024): VAE skill-latent decomposition (H=10 segments composed into full trajectory) as a template for learned low-dim composition if a single global spline is too rigid.

**Key gaps.** None of the candidates close the loop for an UNDERACTUATED deformable tip: every proven B-spline/Bezier/MP decoder (CNP-B, table tennis, quadruped) constrains a RIGID, directly-actuated end-effector where forward kinematics gives the terminal state in closed form — our tip state is the output of a learned, uncertain rope forward model, so closed-form endpoint pinning (BMP's key feature) applies only to the ARM proxy, not the tip; the position+direction guarantee must be re-established through the forward model, which no candidate demonstrates. There is no work parameterizing a compact action for a high-speed BALLISTIC rope-whip with simultaneous tip position AND arrival-direction targeting open-loop (the whip papers target position only, via min-jerk grid optimization, not arrival direction; air-hockey/table-tennis target direction but for rigid bodies). The right via-point count / knot density and basis order for capturing whip wind-and-release dynamics is unmeasured. Whether a single global BMP suffices or a 2-phase (windup+strike) or learned-latent (D-Cubed-style) composition is needed for the full feasibility map is unproven. Finally, no candidate quantifies how compact-action smoothness interacts with the model-exploitation risk under an ensemble — the assumption that a low-dim smooth manifold resists exploitation is plausible (CoRL 2022 trust-region) but untested on deformable dynamics.

**How it helps us.** Adopt BMP (B-spline movement primitives) as the action decoder rather than raw min-jerk knots: it is the only candidate that pins BOTH endpoint position and endpoint velocity in closed form, so the rope-tip arrival-direction constraint is satisfied by construction on the actuated proxy and the optimizer never spends effort fighting boundary penalties. Crucially, do NOT pin the tip directly (the tip is underactuated and lives at the end of the forward model); instead parameterize the ARM joint trajectory as a BMP, and let the planner optimize the low-dimensional Gaussian weight vector so the forward model maps it to the desired tip position+direction — this is the episodic black-box framing (CoRL 2022) that makes our sparse terminal tip-success cost well-shaped where step-based RL fails. Wrap that weight-space search in the differentiable trust-region layer from the same paper, which doubles as the anti-model-exploitation mechanism: a well-conditioned, bounded, smooth low-dim manifold is far harder for the planner to exploit against the ensemble than raw per-timestep actions. Add an explicit preparatory (backward counter-movement) segment to the spline, justified directly by the whip BioRob result that wind-up measurably expands the reachable position workspace — this is where the optional Hopf/limit-cycle pillar plugs into the action representation. Net design: BMP arm-trajectory decoder (with a 2-phase windup+strike via-point layout) → forward model → tip trajectory, optimized in compact Gaussian weight space under a trust region.

### Robust planning vs model exploitation (probabilistic ensemble / pessimism / trust-region; cost-guided diffusion prior; max-margin action selection)

**State of the art.** The strongest current recipe for planning over an imperfect learned forward model is a probabilistic ensemble (PETS) whose disagreement is turned into an explicit pessimism penalty during sampling-based optimization (CEM/MPPI), so the planner is penalized for entering state-action regions where the ensemble disagrees rather than rewarded for whatever the most optimistic member predicts. Two refinements have matured this. (1) Better uncertainty signals: GC-PMPC ("Multi-Goal Dexterous Hand Manipulation", 2025) shows goal-conditioned ensemble MPC where epistemic variance actively suppresses exploitation on a real 12-DOF hand, and the RSS-2023 JRD work replaces raw ensemble variance with Jensen-Renyi divergence between members, giving a tighter, hyperparameter-light pessimism term that doubles as an exploration signal. (2) Better search: BaB-ND (ICLR 2025) abandons CEM/MPPI's vulnerability to spurious optima entirely, adapting neural-network-verification branch-and-bound to globally bound the objective over a frozen neural dynamics model, and it is validated on real deformable rope routing. In parallel, the diffusion-prior camp constrains the search to a learned trajectory manifold so the optimizer cannot wander into physically implausible "model holes": D-Cubed (CEM-inside-reverse-diffusion on deformable tasks), Diffusion-ES (gradient-free evolutionary search with manifold-preserving truncated-noise mutation), MetaDiffuser (dual guidance enforcing dynamics consistency AND return), and KCGG (whole-trajectory gradient guidance applied to the predicted clean sample, with GPU-batch argmin selection). The throwing literature contributes the complementary "succeed across a set, not at a nominal point" idea: Tube Acceleration (T-RO 2024) optimizes a whole family of trajectories that all succeed across a release-timing uncertainty window — a convex max-margin surrogate for pessimism. The honest state of the art is that no single method combines ensemble-pessimism with a manifold-constrained generative prior; each camp has half of the anti-exploitation story.

**Directly usable:**
- PETS probabilistic ensemble + TS-infinity particle propagation as the base forward-model uncertainty estimator (already in wiki: trajectory-sampling-uncertainty-propagation) — feed its per-bootstrap disagreement directly as the pessimism term.
- JRD (Jensen-Renyi divergence) between ensemble members as a drop-in replacement for raw ensemble variance in the planning cost (RSS 2023 'Bridging Active Exploration and Uncertainty-Aware Deployment') — sharper pessimism with no separate conservatism hyperparameter, and reusable to pick informative calibration swings.
- GC-PMPC goal-conditioned ensemble pessimism (Multi-Goal Dexterous Hand 2025) — condition the ensemble on (target position, target direction) and pessimistically reject trajectory parameters where members disagree on the predicted tip endpoint.
- BaB-ND branch-and-bound over the frozen neural forward model (ICLR 2025) — global objective bounding that provably prunes the spurious optima CEM/MPPI exploit; validated on real rope routing and GPU-parallel, directly competitive with our PETS+trust-region planner.
- D-Cubed CEM-inside-reverse-diffusion loop (2024) — embed simulator/ensemble-evaluated cost into the denoising steps so the search stays on a learned trajectory manifold (anti-exploitation by construction); validated on deformable rope task.
- Diffusion-ES manifold-preserving mutation (2024) — gradient-free evolutionary search that wraps our black-box ensemble-pessimism cost while keeping samples on the spline-trajectory manifold; truncated noise-then-denoise mutation replaces structure-destroying Gaussian CEM perturbations.
- MetaDiffuser dual-guided sampling (ICML 2023) — jointly enforce dynamics-consistency AND tip-cost during diffusion; the dynamics-consistency classifier IS the anti-model-exploitation mechanism, and its context-encoder conditioning matches our RMA calibration design.
- KCGG whole-trajectory gradient guidance + GPU-batch argmin (ICRA 2025) — apply cost gradients to the predicted clean trajectory (not the noisy iterate) through forward kinematics + score net, then pick best of a parallel batch by cost; lightweight exploitation-resistant alternative to CEM.
- Tube Acceleration convex 'success tube' (T-RO 2024) — optimize a trajectory family robust across an uncertainty window rather than a single nominal trajectory; a clean max-margin surrogate for pessimism applied to open-loop execution jitter.
- Episodic deep black-box RL with differentiable trust-region layers (CoRL 2022) — a convex-QP trust-region per update step that bounds how far each planning/policy step moves in the compact MP/spline parameter space, directly stabilizing optimization against model exploitation.

**Key gaps.** No candidate combines ensemble-based pessimism with a manifold-constrained generative prior — each camp addresses only one exploitation pathway (bad uncertainty signal vs. off-manifold proposals), and nobody has tested the fusion. All ensemble-pessimism evidence (GC-PMPC, JRD-RSS) is on quasi-static contact (dexterous hand, wheeled robot), NOT high-speed ballistic deformable dynamics where epistemic uncertainty concentrates in the chaotic tip-release phase — it is unproven that ensemble disagreement remains a calibrated trust map for whip-tip trajectories. Critically, every robust planner here is closed-loop online MPC (re-solving each step), so pessimism is cheap insurance that feedback later corrects; OUR setting forbids that — there is zero published evidence on how much pessimism/margin is needed for genuinely open-loop one-shot execution where a single exploited prediction is unrecoverable. The diffusion-prior methods (D-Cubed, Diffusion-ES, KCGG, MetaDiffuser) optimize action SEQUENCES with per-step dynamics, not a one-shot action-to-WHOLE-tip-trajectory forward model, and none enforce a terminal velocity-DIRECTION constraint (only position/return) — direction-cost shaping over an uncertain model is untested. Tube Acceleration's "success tube" is convex only for analytic ballistic flowmaps; whether a tractable success-set exists for whip-tip arrival (position x direction) under model uncertainty is an open problem flagged even in our own pullback-tube wiki page. Finally, no method calibrates how the pessimism penalty weight should be set from the few-minute real calibration residual — the link from calibration uncertainty to planning conservatism is entirely missing.

**How it helps us.** Build the planner as a manifold-constrained search wrapping a pessimistic ensemble cost, because our two named candidate camps (PETS-pessimism and Diffuser-cost-guidance) each fix only half of model exploitation and the candidates show how to fuse them. Concretely: train a diffusion (or BMP-style probabilistic spline) prior over compact min-jerk via-point parameters so the optimizer can only propose physically realizable whip motions (this is what stops CEM/MPPI from diving into "model holes"), then score every candidate with a probabilistic ensemble forward-model cost = tip-position error + arrival-direction error PLUS a JRD ensemble-disagreement penalty, and run the search as Diffusion-ES / KCGG-style manifold-preserving sampling with GPU-batch argmin rather than vanilla CEM. This is decisive for us because we are open-loop and zero-shot: there is NO feedback to recover from an exploited optimistic prediction, so the planner must select the trajectory whose success is robust across the whole ensemble (max-margin / Tube-Acceleration "succeed across the set"), not the one with the best single-model nominal score. Adopt BaB-ND as the falsification baseline — if branch-and-bound's globally-bounded optimum beats our pessimistic sampler on the sim feasibility grid, our pessimism term is mis-specified. The single highest-leverage move is to make the planning cost explicitly penalize ensemble disagreement at the predicted tip endpoint (JRD on the one-shot whole-trajectory output), since that endpoint disagreement is exactly the map of where our sim-prior + few-minute-calibrated model should not be trusted.

- Bridging Active Exploration and Uncertainty-Aware Deployment Using Probabilistic Ensemble Neural Network Dynamics (RSS 2023) — JRD pessimism penalty, drop-in upgrade over ensemble variance.

### Wind-up / limit-cycle start (Hopf bifurcation, rhythmic primitives, entrainment, phase-triggered strike, swing-up / energy-pumping)

**State of the art.** The strongest current evidence for this pillar comes from two complementary 2024-2025 lines. First, biomechanics-grounded whip control (Krotov & Sternad; the BioRob 2024 "Preparatory Movements" paper; the BioRob 2026 center-of-mass internal-model paper) shows empirically that humans solve underactuated flexible-tool targeting by (a) executing a backward counter-movement that pumps energy into the chain before the forward strike, and (b) planning against a single low-dimensional surrogate (the whip/rope center of mass) rather than the full tip state. The BioRob 2024 result is the headline: a backward preparatory phase substantially enlarges the reachable target workspace via stored-then-released energy, and the two-phase motion is well captured by a minimum-jerk profile. Second, on the formal-control side, Orbitally Stable Motion Primitives (OSMPs, 2025) learn a supercritical Hopf normal form (ṙ = α(1-r²/R²)r, φ̇ = f_ω(φ)) inside a diffeomorphic latent space, giving provable limit-cycle convergence and transverse contraction plus a task-conditioning scalar z for zero-shot variation. This is the modern descendant of the classical CPG/Hopf-oscillator and dynamic-movement-primitive literature (Ijspeert, Schaal, Righetti's adaptive-frequency Hopf oscillators, entrainment/phase-resetting), and of energy-pumping swing-up control (e.g. Åström-Furuta energy shaping). The net state of the art: limit-cycle dynamics are a mature, stable way to manufacture a *repeatable, contraction-stable initial condition*, and counter-movement wind-up is experimentally validated to expand whip-tip reachability — but no published system closes the loop from a learned Hopf wind-up into an open-loop, zero-shot, position-plus-direction whip strike.

**Directly usable:**
- OSMP supercritical-Hopf latent (ṙ=α(1-r²/R²)r, φ̇=f_ω): learn a contraction-stable wind-up limit cycle that produces an identical pre-strike state every trial — the repeatable open-loop initial condition our pipeline needs.
- OSMP task-conditioning scalar z + smooth interpolation loss: one calibrated wind-up policy spanning the (position × direction) feasibility map without per-target retraining (zero-shot consistent with our constraint).
- BioRob-2024 backward counter-movement as a prior: prepend an energy-pumping back-swing phase to the spline so the forward strike inherits stored momentum — quantifies the reachability lost if wind-up is omitted.
- Two-phase minimum-jerk (backward-then-forward) via-point layout from BioRob-2024: a human-grounded warm-start for our min-jerk via-point spline decoder.
- Phase-triggered strike: treat the Hopf phase φ as the trigger clock — release the strike trajectory at a fixed φ so the forward swing always launches from the same energy state, removing wind-up timing jitter from the open-loop schedule.
- Adaptive-frequency Hopf oscillator (Righetti/Ijspeert) for calibration: let the oscillator entrain to the real rope's natural sway frequency during the few-minute calibration, encoding a rope-specific resonant wind-up frequency into the frozen policy.
- Center-of-mass surrogate (BioRob 2026 / Krotov-Sternad): plan the wind-up and strike against the rope COM as a low-D proxy, simplifying the feasibility-map search.

**Key gaps.** OSMP and the CPG/Hopf literature are about *sustained periodic* motion, but our task is fundamentally aperiodic: a single ballistic strike. Nothing in the candidate set demonstrates a clean, stable *transition* from a limit-cycle attractor into a one-shot ballistic throw, nor characterizes how transverse-contraction guarantees survive that hand-off — the limit cycle buys a repeatable initial condition but says nothing about the open-loop strike's accuracy. None of the wind-up work co-optimizes for an *arrival direction* (unit velocity vector) at the tip; OSMP reproduces trajectories and the whip-bio papers optimize tip-to-target *distance* only, so direction control is unproven for this pillar. The BioRob counter-movement evidence is on a tabletop 25-DOF whip targeting a spatial grid, not 3D position+direction, and is open-loop only implicitly. Crucially, the wind-up amplifies the very anti-model-exploitation risk our planning pillar fears: energy pumping makes the tip state at strike highly sensitive to small dynamics errors, yet no candidate quantifies wind-up robustness under model uncertainty or sim-to-real on a high-speed dynamic rope (OSMP's hardware is slow/quasi-rhythmic UR5/soft-helix). Finally, there is no demonstration that a learned Hopf wind-up *frequency/amplitude* can be set zero-shot from a few-minute real calibration on a new rope.

**How it helps us.** Adopt wind-up as a deliberate *initial-condition factory*, not as the controller: use an OSMP-style supercritical-Hopf limit cycle to drive the arm into a contraction-stable, phase-defined pre-strike state, then trigger the open-loop min-jerk via-point strike at a fixed Hopf phase φ. This directly solves the repeatability problem our open-loop/zero-shot constraint demands — every execution launches the ballistic phase from the *same* energy state, so the forward model only has to predict the strike from one canonical starting condition rather than from arbitrary transient poses, which shrinks the forward model's input distribution and reduces compounding error. Concretely, fold the wind-up into the action parameterization as two appended via-point segments (backward energy-pump, then forward strike) per the BioRob-2024 min-jerk structure, with the back-swing amplitude/frequency conditioned on the RMA calibration latent so a single frozen policy spans the feasibility map. Be opinionated about scope: keep the Hopf machinery for the *windup-and-launch* sub-phase only and let the robust ensemble/diffusion planner own the ballistic strike — and treat wind-up as optional/ablatable, because its energy amplification is double-edged: it expands reachability (BioRob-2024) but magnifies model-exploitation sensitivity, so it must be planned *inside* the pessimistic cost, never bolted on after.

### Sim-to-real and offline calibration for fast DLO motion (offline DR posterior / DROPO / BayesSim / SimOpt; residual-hybrid dynamics; GPU rope-sim fidelity at high tip speed)

**State of the art.** The field has converged on offline, calibration-time inference of a low-dimensional physical-parameter vector (rope stiffness, damping, linear density, link count, tip-lead mass) followed by frozen deployment, which is exactly our regime. Three families dominate. (1) Bayesian / likelihood-free posterior inference (BayesSim, DROPO, and the "Bayesian Treatment of Real-to-Sim for Deformable Objects" candidate) recovers a full posterior over physical params from a short real rollout and uses it as grounded domain randomization rather than a point estimate — important because rope dynamics are genuinely multi-modal/under-determined. (2) Task-driven simulator adaptation (AdaptSim, and the ASID active-exploration line) tunes sim params to maximize downstream task accuracy or Fisher information rather than raw trajectory-matching, getting more transfer per real second. (3) Amortized / in-context system-ID (Dynamics-as-Prompts SysID transformer, RMA-style context encoders, MAVEN's predictive encoder) replaces gradient-based calibration with a single forward pass that maps a real probe rollout to params or a latent, then freezes. For DLOs specifically, the strongest current points are Wiggle-and-Go (task-agnostic wiggle-probe sysID -> 9-D rope vector -> open-loop strike, already in our wiki), R2S2R / planar robot casting (Differential-Evolution sim-tuning from ~20 real trajectories, mixed sim+real forward model), and DER-in-MuJoCo generalized-coordinate calibration (physically-faithful bending/twist with a 2-experiment ID pipeline). Residual-hybrid dynamics (residual network on top of a physics simulator, as in the spin-residual table-tennis and residual-physics work) is the standard patch for the last-mile sim-real gap on the predicted tip trajectory. The open frontier is GPU-native deformable simulators (FLASH, Isaac/FleX-segmented cable) that make millions of high-speed rope rollouts cheap enough to train the calibrator and the forward model entirely in sim.

**Directly usable:**
- AdaptSim (CoRL 2023): task-driven sim-parameter adaptation — calibrate sim for tip position/direction error, not raw trajectory-matching; drop-in template for the few-minute real specialization loop.
- ASID (ICLR 2024): Fisher-information active-exploration to DESIGN the calibration probe motion in sim, so one short real swing maximally identifies rope stiffness/damping — cuts real calibration data toward a single rollout.
- Dynamics-as-Prompts SysID transformer (RA-L 2024): gradient-free in-context calibration — read minutes of real tip trajectories, output corrected sim params in one forward pass; cleaner than RMA when you want explicit params (code available).
- Bayesian Treatment of Real-to-Sim for Deformable Objects (RA-L/ICRA 2022): likelihood-free posterior over rope elasticity/friction with distribution-embedding summary stats; reuse the posterior as grounded DR and the embedding as the context-encoder observation features.
- R2S2R / planar robot casting Differential-Evolution sim-tuning (ICRA 2022, in-wiki): fit Isaac/FleX-segmented cable params from ~20 real free-end-cable trajectories — proven recipe for our per-rope tuning baseline.
- DER-in-MuJoCo generalized-coordinate model + 2-experiment ID pipeline (IROS 2025, in-wiki): physically-faithful bending/twist rope sim with ~1-3% overhead — candidate high-fidelity forward simulator with a hardware-light alpha=EI, beta=GJ calibration.
- MAVEN predictive context encoder (2026): permutation-invariant MLP over a short transition buffer with explicit dynamics+reward prediction loss — architecture pattern for the frozen-after-calibration RMA encoder, GPU-parallel trained.
- FLASH GPU-native deformable solver (2026) / Isaac-FleX segmented cable: massively-parallel rope rollouts to train the calibrator and one-shot forward model entirely in sim before the real probe.
- Incremental few-shot physics-param adaptation (ICRA 2025): converges in <10 real interactions without full sysID — fallback if amortized encoder under-specializes a given rope.

**Key gaps.** No existing calibration method targets the HIGH-TIP-SPEED ballistic rope regime: published DLO real-to-sim work (Bayesian deformable, DiffCloud, DER-MuJoCo) calibrates from quasi-static or low-speed shape data, where bending/twist stiffness dominates; at whip speeds inertial, drag, and damping params dominate and are largely unidentified by slow probes — so a wiggle probe may not excite the modes that matter for the throw. No method jointly calibrates for BOTH position AND arrival-direction accuracy; all DLO sysID validates on tip position (Wiggle-and-Go, R2S2R) and ignores tip-velocity-direction error, which is more sensitive to damping/drag mismatch. The posterior-aware pipeline is unproven end-to-end: BayesSim/DROPO give a posterior but no DLO paper feeds that posterior into a pessimistic one-shot open-loop planner — the anti-exploitation handoff is conceptual, not demonstrated. Calibration-vs-execution mismatch is unaddressed: a low-amplitude probe identifies the rope in a different dynamic regime than the high-energy throw (and from a different initial condition than a wound-up Hopf limit cycle), and no work quantifies this regime-shift error. Finally, residual-hybrid dynamics for fast rope tip trajectories is untested — residual heads are validated on table-tennis ball flight and quasi-static deformables, not on a one-shot whole-tip-trajectory prediction at high speed.

**How it helps us.** Adopt a two-stage calibration that is offline-posterior, not point-estimate: a wiggle/whip probe rollout (captured by your motion capture) feeds an amortized in-context encoder (Dynamics-as-Prompts transformer or RMA-style encoder trained in the GPU sim under domain randomization) that outputs a posterior over rope params; then freeze. Critically, do NOT calibrate by trajectory-matching alone — make the calibration task-driven (AdaptSim) and information-optimal (ASID Fisher-information probe design), because for the high-tip-speed regime the params that matter for hitting a target-position-plus-direction are not the same as the params that minimize average rope-shape error. Then carry the posterior (not a single param point) into the PETS-style planner so pessimism is computed over genuine calibration uncertainty, and add a residual-physics correction head on the predicted tip trajectory to absorb the last-mile sim-real gap at high speed. This is the single highest-leverage move: it directly attacks model-exploitation (the dominant risk) at its source — the planner is then pessimistic over a real, observation-grounded parameter posterior rather than an arbitrary hand-set domain-randomization range.

### The task itself: open-loop zero-shot rope/whip tip targeting to POSITION + DIRECTION (closest existing systems; direction-conditioned hitting/striking)

**State of the art.** The published rope/whip task literature we already know (IRP, DIDP, IPA, Wiggle-and-Go, Flying-Knot-ILC, Lost-ARC) is almost entirely POSITION-ONLY: every one targets a 3D tip point and none specifies an arrival DIRECTION, so the closest existing systems for the position+direction variant come from the rigid striking/hitting domain rather than from DLOs. There, the state of the art is mature: air-hockey and table-tennis systems routinely impose, as explicit terminal constraints in a single optimization, the contact position, the velocity DIRECTION (angle), and even the surface normal — see CNP-B (constraint-manifold + B-spline decoder, single forward pass), FHMPC table tennis (terminal position + velocity-direction-angle + paddle-orientation constraints solved in ~3 ms), Spot table tennis (SQP aiming that inverts paddle position+velocity-direction+normal from a desired landing point), and air-hockey contact-planning (collapse to a 1D shooting-angle action distilled into an EBM). These systems all separate an aiming sub-problem (invert desired endpoint position+direction into an endpoint robot/tool state) from a trajectory sub-problem (find a smooth, kinematically feasible motion realizing that endpoint), and they all use compact spline/movement-primitive decoders (B-spline, Bezier) whose boundary conditions enforce arrival velocity exactly. On the whip side specifically, the newest human-inspired work (Edraki "Preparatory Movements Improve Hitting a Target," BioRob 2024; the 2026 center-of-mass internal-model whip study) shows preparatory wind-up dramatically expands the reachable TARGET workspace and that a low-dimensional surrogate (whip CoM) suffices as a planning proxy — but these are open-loop, position-only, and human-subject/simulation studies, not zero-shot direction-conditioned robot systems. The crucial gap: no system unifies (a) a deformable underactuated rope tip, (b) BOTH position and arrival direction, (c) pure open-loop zero-shot execution after one-time calibration — the rope work drops direction and the striking work is rigid-body and usually closed-loop MPC.

**Directly usable:**
- CNP-B (T-RO 2024): learned constraint-manifold + B-spline decoder mapping a (target-position, velocity-direction) endpoint to a feasible joint trajectory in one forward pass — direct blueprint for our non-autoregressive decoder AND our feasibility map of reachable (position x direction) pairs.
- FHMPC table tennis (2025): terminal OCP constraints stated SEPARATELY on arrival position, velocity-direction ANGLE, and surface orientation — copy this constraint structure verbatim into our tip-target cost (speed magnitude left free, matching our spec).
- Spot table tennis SQP aimer (ICRA 2026): invert a desired endpoint (position + velocity direction + normal) into a tool state in ~1 ms via SQP, then realize it with an 8-control-point Bezier per joint — template for our aiming-then-trajectory split.
- BMP (2024): B-spline-as-movement-primitive with CLOSED-FORM pinning of both endpoint position AND velocity — the exact decoder we need so arrival direction is a hard boundary condition, not a penalty.
- Edraki et al. 'Preparatory Movements Improve Hitting a Target' (BioRob 2024): min-jerk two-phase (backward wind-up then forward strike) on a real 25-DOF whip; quantifies that wind-up expands the reachable target workspace — empirical justification for our optional Hopf wind-up and a via-point prior.
- Air-hockey contact-planning distillation (RSS 2025): collapse the high-dim trajectory space to a 1D shooting-angle that encodes desired contact velocity direction, then sample-evaluate-distill — pattern for compressing our (position,direction) planning into a tractable task-relevant action.
- Whip center-of-mass internal model (BioRob 2026): a single low-D surrogate (whip CoM) is a sufficient planning proxy for the high-DOF tip — motivates a compressed latent for both the forward model output and the feasibility map.

**Key gaps.** No existing system targets a DEFORMABLE rope tip with BOTH position and arrival direction under open-loop zero-shot execution: the rope/whip task work (IRP, DIDP, IPA, Wiggle-and-Go, Flying-Knot) is position-only, and the only systems that condition on velocity direction (air hockey, table tennis) act on RIGID end-effectors/paddles whose velocity at contact is a near-direct function of joint state, whereas a rope tip's arrival direction is the emergent product of a traveling-wave/underactuated chain delayed in time from any joint motion — so the clean rigid aiming inversion does NOT transfer and there is no published validation that arrival DIRECTION of a free rope tip can be controlled open-loop at all. The direction-conditioned systems are also almost all closed-loop MPC (FHMPC, Spot WB-MPC, contact-planning MPC handoff, MPPI-IsaacGym) or rely on a real-time simulator-as-oracle, none demonstrating the no-feedback, frozen-after-calibration regime we require. Feasibility maps in the striking literature (CNP-B's constraint manifold) cover rigid reachable position+velocity sets; the analogous reachable set for a rope tip including direction — and how much the wind-up phase expands it — is unquantified. Finally, none of these systems demonstrates ZERO-SHOT generalization to arbitrary new (position, direction) targets after a one-time per-object calibration; CNP-B trains a fixed-rope manifold offline, table tennis systems re-solve per shot online, and the whip studies are single-physical-whip case studies.

**How it helps us.** Adopt the air-hockey/table-tennis "aiming + trajectory" decomposition as the explicit structure of OUR cost, not just as inspiration: define the success cost as a terminal constraint on (tip position, tip velocity DIRECTION) exactly as FHMPC and the Spot SQP aimer do, and feed it to the forward model that predicts the WHOLE tip trajectory in one shot so the direction term is read off the predicted tip velocity at the arrival instant. The single most actionable move is to make arrival DIRECTION a first-class boundary condition rather than a soft penalty — use a BMP/B-spline action decoder (BMP pins both endpoint position AND velocity in closed form) so that the JOINT-space action's terminal tip behavior is shaped toward the right direction by construction, shrinking the planner's search space and directly attacking model-exploitation by removing the degenerate "hit the point from any direction" optima that a position-only cost admits. Concretely: parameterize the throw as min-jerk via-points -> BMP control points (endpoint velocity pinned), score candidates with the ensemble forward model under a position+direction+speed-agnostic cost, and add a CNP-B-style learned feasibility manifold so the planner only proposes (position, direction) pairs that are reachable — this also gives us the feasibility map deliverable for free. Borrow the wind-up evidence from Edraki et al. to justify the optional Hopf pre-throw: it is the mechanism that makes far/awkward-direction targets reachable at all, so the feasibility map should be computed WITH the preparatory phase enabled.
