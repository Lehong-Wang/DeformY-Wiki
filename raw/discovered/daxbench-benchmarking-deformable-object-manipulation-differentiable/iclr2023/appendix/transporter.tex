\section{Transporter with Image as Input}\label{sec:transporter}
We reported the performance of two different versions of Transporter in Table \ref{table:transporter}: Transporter, which uses 3D particles and projects them onto an image to form a height map; and Transporter-RGB, which adds RGB channels and projects the particles onto the image plane, giving it white color with a black background. We have found that an explicit process of rendering particles into RGB images significantly enhances the performance of Transporter.
