# Tong et al. 2024 — Sim2Real Neural Controllers for Physics-Based Robotic Deployment of DLOs

**One-line gist**: Sim2real neural controller for DLO deployment using physics-based DER models from the Jawed group.

**Task setup**: Robotic DLO manipulation tasks (deployment, shaping) with a sim2real neural controller.

**Sim vs real**: Sim2real, with DER-based simulator and real-robot deployment.

**Learning method**: Neural controllers trained against a DER physics simulator and transferred to real hardware.

**Action representation**: Robot end-effector commands.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Cited in Report 1 as a Jawed-group sim2real follow-up that grounds the DER-physics + neural control recipe used in DEFORM/DEFT and DisMech-rl.

**Key result (if any)**: Sim2real DLO deployment demonstrated in IJRR 2024.
