# Dynamic Throwing with Robotic Material Handling Machines

**One-line gist**: 12-ton hydraulic excavator with a passive-joint gripper learns dynamic throwing of objects to targets outside its static reach via sim-to-real RL that exploits passive-joint dynamics.

**Task setup**: 12-ton hydraulic excavator-style underactuated arm with a passive-joint gripper throws objects to 3D targets located outside the static reach zone.

**Sim vs real**: Sim-to-real RL controller, validated on the real excavator.

**Learning method**: RL controller exploiting passive-joint dynamics for amplification; sim-to-real.

**Action representation**: Joint-velocity trajectory.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Cited in Report 1 as a striking example of underactuated/passive-joint dynamic throwing — methodologically related to whip-targeting because both rely on passive-element dynamics for tip-velocity amplification.

**Key result (if any)**: Throws individual objects accurately to targets outside static reach.
