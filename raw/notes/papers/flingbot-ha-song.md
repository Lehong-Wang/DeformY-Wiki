# FlingBot — Dynamic Manipulation for Cloth Unfolding

**One-line gist**: Pick-stretch-fling primitive for dynamic cloth manipulation; methodological inspiration for rope-flicking but not goal-conditioned to a 3D point.

**Task setup**: Cloth unfolding via a dual-arm pick-stretch-fling primitive.

**Sim vs real**: Self-supervised on real hardware + simulation.

**Learning method**: Self-supervised learning of fling parameters.

**Action representation**: Pick + stretch + fling primitive.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Cited in Report 1 as one of the methodological analogs whose pick-stretch-fling primitive and self-supervised framework directly inspired rope-flicking work. Same Columbia group as IRP. Cloth, not rope; not goal-conditioned to 3D points.

**Key result (if any)**: Demonstrated dramatic improvements in cloth unfolding via dynamic flinging.
