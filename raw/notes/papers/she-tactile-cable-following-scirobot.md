# Cable Manipulation with a Tactile-Reactive Gripper

**One-line gist**: Closed-loop tactile control for cable following with a tactile-reactive gripper; landmark for tactile sensing in cable manipulation.

**Task setup**: Cable following / sliding along a cable using a tactile-reactive gripper. Quasi-static.

**Sim vs real**: Real-world with GelSight-style tactile sensing.

**Learning method**: Closed-loop tactile control (perceived tactile flow → gripper adjustments); not RL.

**Action representation**: Continuous gripper-pose adjustments driven by tactile feedback.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Landmark Science Robotics paper on tactile sensing for cable manipulation. Cited as evidence that tactile signals can replace or augment vision for cable-following, complementing the vision-driven dynamic-DLO line.

**Key result (if any)**: Reliable closed-loop cable following on real cables of varying stiffness.
