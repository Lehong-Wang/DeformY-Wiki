# Choi et al. 2021 — Implicit Contact Model for DER in Knot Tying

**One-line gist**: Implicit-contact DER formulation that handles self-contact stably during knot tying; enables faithful knot-tying simulation in the DER framework.

**Task setup**: Simulating knot tying with self-contact between rod segments.

**Sim vs real**: Sim-only.

**Learning method**: None.

**Action representation**: N/A.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Cited in Report 1 as a key extension of DER for self-contact / knot tying. Important enabling tool for any DLO simulator that must handle dense-knot configurations, even though knotting is not the primary tip-target task.

**Key result (if any)**: Implicit-contact DER stable for knot-tying simulation.
