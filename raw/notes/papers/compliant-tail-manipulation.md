# Using a Compliant, Unactuated Tail to Manipulate Objects

**One-line gist**: A robot uses a compliant, unactuated rope-like tail to strike objects of interest at high speed; planning via RRT with a particle-based uncertainty representation.

**Task setup**: A robot with a compliant rope-like tail (unactuated appendage) strikes objects of interest at high speed. Planning frames the appendage as a flexible striker.

**Sim vs real**: Real-robot demonstrations cited.

**Learning method**: None — classical planning.

**Action representation**: Sampling-based planning over the robot's actuated body, with the compliant tail's motion induced from base motion.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Cited in Report 3 as one of the cleanest task formulations of "flexible appendage used as a dynamic striker." Methodologically distinct from gripping-the-rope-tip work, but a direct conceptual ancestor of dynamic tip-targeting.

**Key result (if any)**: Demonstrated dynamic high-speed striking of target objects via a compliant tail.
