# In-Air Knotting of Rope using Dual-Arm Robot based on Deep Learning

**One-line gist**: Deep-learning sensorimotor policy for dual-arm in-air rope knotting.

**Task setup**: Dual-arm robot tying knots in a rope held mid-air.

**Sim vs real**: Real-world dual-arm hardware with deep-learned sensorimotor control.

**Learning method**: Deep sensorimotor learning, not goal-conditioned to a 3D point.

**Action representation**: Joint or end-effector control via the learned policy.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Cited in Report 1 as a deep-learning analog within the in-air knotting / dynamic rope manipulation line; included for completeness as one of the few learning-based dual-arm rope-manipulation systems.

**Key result (if any)**: Demonstrated dual-arm in-air rope knotting via deep learning.
