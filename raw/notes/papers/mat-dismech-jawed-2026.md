# MAT-DiSMech (MATLAB) and Py-DiSMech (Python)

**One-line gist**: MATLAB-native and Python-native re-implementations of the DisMech DDG rod/shell simulator, expanding accessibility of high-fidelity DER physics.

**Task setup**: Simulation of rods, shells, and combined structures using DDG-based DER physics with implicit time integration. MAT-DiSMech is the MATLAB version (J. Robotics Research 2026); Py-DiSMech (arXiv 2512.09911) is the scalable Python version.

**Sim vs real**: Sim-only frameworks.

**Learning method**: None — physics simulators.

**Action representation**: N/A.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Cited in Report 1 as the next-generation accessible DDG/DER simulators from the Jawed group, complementing DisMech-rods (C++) by providing MATLAB and Python entrypoints.

**Key result (if any)**: Higher-fidelity, accessible-language reimplementations of the DisMech DER simulator.
