# CoRdE — Cosserat Rod Elements for the Dynamic Simulation of One-Dimensional Elastic Objects

**One-line gist**: Foundational FEM Cosserat rod simulator with continuous bending/twisting/stretching energies; CPU; not differentiable; reference physics for many later DLO sim2real works.

**Task setup**: Simulator (not a task) for the dynamic simulation of one-dimensional elastic objects (ropes, cables, surgical sutures).

**Sim vs real**: Sim only.

**Learning method**: None — simulator.

**Action representation**: N/A.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Cited in Reports 2 and 3 as the canonical Cosserat-rod simulator companion to DER in the dynamic-DLO line. Standard reference for many later DLO sim2real works.

**Key result (if any)**: Stable elastic-rod dynamics with self-contact; standard reference.
