# DextAIRity — Deformable Manipulation with Air

**One-line gist**: Uses an air-blower as an additional actuator for dynamic cloth/deformable manipulation; sister Columbia group paper to IRP, demonstrating self-supervised dynamic deformable manipulation.

**Task setup**: Cloth and deformable manipulation tasks where an air blower is treated as an actuated tool alongside the robot arm.

**Sim vs real**: Sim + real-world.

**Learning method**: Self-supervised learning over the joint action space (arm + blower).

**Action representation**: Combined arm and blower commands.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Cited in Report 1 as a sister paper to IRP from the same Columbia group, demonstrating a different style of dynamic deformable manipulation. Cloth, not rope; not 3D-tip-conditioned.

**Key result (if any)**: Demonstrated dynamic deformable manipulation with combined arm + blower actuation.
