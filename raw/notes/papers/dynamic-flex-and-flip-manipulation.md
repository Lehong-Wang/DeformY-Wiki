# Dynamic Flex-and-Flip Manipulation of Deformable Linear Objects

**One-line gist**: Dynamic two-finger flipping primitive that exploits inertial effects of a DLO to acquire a grasp; quasi-dynamic, but uses inertia rather than quasi-static contact.

**Task setup**: Two-finger flip of a DLO to acquire a grasp; uses dynamic inertial effects of the cable.

**Sim vs real**: Not extracted from sources; included primarily as a dynamic-DLO methodological reference.

**Learning method**: Method-paper detail not extracted; cited as a representative quasi-dynamic flex-and-flip primitive in the dynamic-DLO line.

**Action representation**: Two-finger flipping motion primitive.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Listed in Report 1 as an adjacent dynamic-DLO example that uses inertial effects, distinct from quasi-static manipulation primitives. Not a goal-conditioned tip-target paper.

**Key result (if any)**: Demonstrates a dynamic flex-and-flip primitive for grasp acquisition on DLOs.
