# Nah et al. 2020 — Dynamic Primitives Facilitate Manipulating a Whip

**One-line gist**: Earlier (2020 BioRob) Sternad/Hogan paper showing dynamic primitives (submovements + impedance) are sufficient to manipulate a whip; precursor to Nah 2023 iScience.

**Task setup**: Whip manipulation modeled with dynamic motor primitives (Sternad/Hogan framework). Predecessor experimental setup to Nah 2023.

**Sim vs real**: Mostly simulation in the Sternad/Hogan series; physical whip experiments reported in companion papers.

**Learning method**: None — model-based dynamic primitives + impedance.

**Action representation**: Submovement primitives + mechanical impedance.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Cited in Report 1 as part of the Sternad/Hogan whip-control cluster (Krotov 2022, Nah 2023, Edraki 2024) and the 2020 entry in that line. Establishes the dynamic-primitives framing that Nah 2023 (iScience) and Edraki 2024 (BioRob) extend with concrete optimization.

**Key result (if any)**: Demonstrated that constant joint mechanical impedances + a single discrete submovement suffice to manipulate a whip.
