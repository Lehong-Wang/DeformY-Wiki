# Dynamic Handover: Throw and Catch with Bimanual Hands

**One-line gist**: Multi-agent RL in sim with sim2real for dynamic throw-and-catch of diverse objects between two dexterous hands.

**Task setup**: One dexterous hand throws and another catches diverse objects at high speed.

**Sim vs real**: Trained in simulation and transferred to real robots.

**Learning method**: Multi-agent reinforcement learning + a learned object trajectory prediction model that helps the catcher estimate where the object is heading.

**Action representation**: Learned arm/hand control policies; specific action parameterization not extracted in the source.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Cited in Reports 1 and 3 as a recent example of dynamic, high-speed, goal-sensitive manipulation with learned perception and sim-to-real transfer. Methodological analog for goal-conditioned dynamic motion involving learned perception of fast-moving objects.

**Key result (if any)**: Bimanual sim2real throw-and-catch on diverse objects.
