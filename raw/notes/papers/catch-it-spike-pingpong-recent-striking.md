# Recent Striking/Catching Analogs Cluster

**One-line gist**: Cluster of recent (2024–2026) RL or imitation-with-motion-priors systems for high-speed striking/catching tasks (Catch It!, SpikePingpong, RAI Institute robot baseball, Humanoid Goalkeeper, Humanoid Whole-Body Badminton). Useful for action-representation comparisons.

**Task setup**: Diverse striking and catching tasks: catching balls/objects, spiking ping-pong, baseball-style hitting, humanoid goalkeeping/badminton. All require high-speed dynamic manipulation against (typically moving) targets.

**Sim vs real**: Most use Isaac Gym training with PPO; sim-to-real transfer.

**Learning method**: RL or imitation-with-motion-priors with PPO in Isaac Gym.

**Action representation**: Joint-space or end-effector commands; varies per system.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Cited in Report 1 as a 2025 cluster of striking/catching analogs that share architectural patterns (PPO + Isaac Gym + motion priors). Useful for comparing action representations across the dynamic-manipulation goal-conditioned space, even if no single member directly tackles rope-tip targeting.

**Key result (if any)**: Various — high-speed striking/catching demonstrations across the cluster.
