# DMMP: Differentiable Motion Manifold Primitives

**One-line gist**: 7-DoF dynamic ball-throwing to arbitrary 3D target points using a continuous-time differentiable manifold of trajectories.

**Task setup**: Throwing a ball to arbitrary 3D target points with a 7-DoF robot arm.

**Sim vs real**: Not extracted in detail; methodological focus is on the differentiable manifold representation.

**Learning method**: Encodes a continuous-time differentiable manifold of throwing trajectories; supports goal conditioning by querying points on the manifold.

**Action representation**: Continuous-time trajectory parameterized by points on a differentiable manifold.

**Why cited in the surveys**: Same methodological flavor as DA-MMP — latent motion-manifold action representations for goal-conditioned dynamic manipulation. Cited as a 2024 entry in the manifold-primitive line.

**Key result (if any)**: 7-DoF ball-throwing to arbitrary 3D target points using DMMP.
